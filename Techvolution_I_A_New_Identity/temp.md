
## Temp

A new identity isn't about what to do. It's about removing the mental roadblock in the way of what we naturally do, and letting evolution take it from there.

Watching TV won't change entropy's attacks. Believing in an ideology won't assemble matter into useful configurations.

Want to be healthier? Follow good nutrition and exercise. The flavour of the food being spicy or savory, the kind of movements being yoga or jiu-jitsu doesn't matter to the final product of a healthy human body.

Likewise, only an ecosystem of beings, looking to harvest energy in different ways, can build matter enough to counter entropy. Responding to environmental challenges and opportunities faster will alleviate entropy. And you can only change your conduct, and look for other tools and people who will form a bigger and faster ecosystem.

We already do this but expect ideology to define who our friends and enemies are. For example, we expect historical identities to define who our allies are, even when we no longer use each other's products—and so no longer share the interests that lifeforms depending on the same ecosystem actually have.
