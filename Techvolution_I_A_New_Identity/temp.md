Intake. Store. Keep.

Sunlight.

Split. Split. Split. Many split.

Silicon thins. Nitrogen thins.

Cannot build shell. Cannot split.

Sunlight less. Clump. Sink.

Sunlight gone.

Dark. Deep. Wait.
